Biography & Experience

About Me

I am Shimul Hossain, actively dedicated to child rights, youth development, and positive social transformation in Debhata, Satkhira, Bangladesh.

Currently, through various programs at Breaking the Silence, I work to foster child and youth participation, leadership development, life skills enhancement, and meaningful community engagement.

Under the SPiRiT (Sports for Protection, Resilience and Transformation) project, I have extensive hands-on experience facilitating the ENGAGE Curriculum, youth-led initiatives, awareness campaigns, training workshops, and grassroots sports sessions.

Alongside my social work, I am deeply passionate about photography, nature, travel, digital content creation, and continuous learning. My mission is to combine my field experience, creativity, and skills to drive lasting positive change in society.
